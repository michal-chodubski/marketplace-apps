Adminbolt logo - required formats
=================================

Akamai requires vector logo files of the Adminbolt app, in both
white and full-color variants. Accepted formats: SVG, EPS, or AI.

Please obtain from the Adminbolt brand team and place here as:

  assets/adminbolt-logo-color.svg
  assets/adminbolt-logo-white.svg

The PNG at https://adminbolt.com/img/logo.png is the current public
mark but is rasterized and not acceptable for the Marketplace
submission as-is.
