Adminbolt brand colors (for Akamai Marketplace gradient hero)
=============================================================

Brand Color 1 (left side of gradient): #F4A23C
  Warm orange taken from the Adminbolt lightning bolt mark.

Brand Color 2 (right side of gradient): #1A1A1A
  Near-black, matches the Adminbolt website background.

Notes:
- The gradient goes left -> right (Color 1 -> Color 2).
- If Color 2 is left empty, Akamai substitutes black, which is acceptable.
